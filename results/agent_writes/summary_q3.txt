In Q3, the Northeast Division's revenue reached 4.2 million dollars (up 11 percent from Q2, driven by mid-market renewals), while overall headcount closed the quarter at 63 full-time employees—a net increase of four, including two new support hires that helped improve median first-response time to 4.4 hours despite an 18 percent rise in support ticket volume. Operations headcount specifically stood at 21 at quarter end after one departure and backfill.
