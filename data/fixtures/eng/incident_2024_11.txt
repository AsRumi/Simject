Incident Report INC-4471

Severity: 2
Duration: 47 minutes
Customers affected: 312

Summary. Requests to the checkout path began returning 503 responses at 14:12
UTC. The root cause was connection pool exhaustion in the billing-api service.
A configuration change deployed earlier that day reduced the pool ceiling from
200 to 20 connections, and normal afternoon traffic saturated it.

Detection came from synthetic checks rather than from alerting on pool
saturation, which is why time to detection was 9 minutes rather than under one.

Resolution. The on-call engineer reverted the configuration change and restarted
the billing-api pods in a rolling fashion. Error rates returned to baseline by
14:59 UTC.
