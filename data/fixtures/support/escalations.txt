Escalation Policy - ESC-88

Tiers. Tier one is the assigned support engineer. Tier two is the support lead
plus the owning engineering team. Tier three adds the account executive and a
director, and is reserved for cases with revenue or contractual exposure.

Ownership rotation. The tier two rotation changes weekly on Monday morning. The
outgoing owner is responsible for a written handoff on any case older than seven
days; a verbal handoff is not sufficient and has been the cause of two dropped
escalations this year.

Aging. Any escalation older than thirty days must be reviewed in the weekly
account health meeting, regardless of tier. This rule exists because escalation
age turned out to predict churn better than any usage metric.
