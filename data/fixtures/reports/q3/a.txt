Q3 Financial Summary - Northeast Division

Revenue for the third quarter reached 4.2 million dollars, up 11 percent from
Q2. The gain came almost entirely from renewals in the mid-market segment;
new-business bookings were flat.

Headcount closed the quarter at 63 full-time employees, a net increase of four.
Two of the four hires were in support, reflecting the renewal-heavy mix.

Gross margin held at 71 percent. No material one-time items.
