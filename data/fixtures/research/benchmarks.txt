Benchmark Results - Internal Evaluation

Dataset DS-19 was used for all runs reported here. It contains 4,000 held-out
examples and was frozen before any of the variants below were trained.

The best performing configuration is model variant V3-b, which reached a score
of 0.874. The next closest was V3-a at 0.861, and the previous generation
baseline sits at 0.792.

The gap between V3-a and V3-b is almost entirely on the long-input subset. On
short inputs the two are within noise of each other, which suggests the
improvement comes from the context handling change rather than from the
additional training data.

Every number here is a single-seed run. Variance across seeds has not yet been
measured, so the ranking between V3-a and V3-b should be treated as provisional.
