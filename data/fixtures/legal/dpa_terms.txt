Data Processing Addendum - key terms, DPA-771

Roles. Customer is controller, we are processor. Processing is limited to the
documented purposes in the underlying agreement; there is no independent right
to process for our own purposes.

Breach notification. We must notify the controller without undue delay and in
any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach. The clock
starts at awareness, not at confirmation of scope.

Subprocessors. The current list is the cloud hosting provider, the transactional
email provider, and the error tracking service. Adding a subprocessor requires
thirty days notice and gives the controller a right to object.

Deletion. On termination, data is deleted within ninety days unless retention is
required by law. Backups follow the standard backup expiry rather than immediate
deletion.
