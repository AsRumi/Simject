Renewals Outlook

Nine renewals are due before the end of the quarter, together worth 1.4 million
dollars in annual recurring revenue.

The at-risk account is Halcyon Freight, renewal ID REN-3120. Churn risk is
assessed at 22 percent, driven by two unresolved support escalations and a
change of economic buyer in August. The account team has requested an executive
sponsor call before the renewal conversation.

The remaining eight are tracking normally. Three have already returned signed
paperwork ahead of schedule.

The pattern worth noting is that every at-risk renewal this year had an open
escalation older than thirty days at the time of assessment. Escalation age is
a better churn predictor than usage decline for this segment.
