Q4 Operating Plan - Northeast Division

Target revenue for the fourth quarter is 4.8 million dollars. The plan assumes
the mid-market renewal rate holds at 91 percent and that two enterprise deals
currently in late stage close before the end of December.

The hiring plan adds 6 people: three in engineering, two in support, one in
sales operations. All six are backfills or previously approved roles; no new
headcount was requested beyond the approved envelope.

The quarter's single largest initiative is the reporting warehouse migration,
tracked as project code PRJ-8814. It carries a hard dependency on the finance
close calendar and cannot start before the second week of October.
