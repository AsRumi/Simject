Q4 Risk Register - Northeast Division

The top risk this quarter is further slippage of the reporting warehouse
migration, recorded in the register as RSK-102. The risk owner is Dana
Whitfield. Current assessment is medium likelihood, high impact, because three
downstream dashboards depend on the migrated schema.

Second risk is concentration: the two late-stage enterprise deals together
represent 31 percent of the quarterly target. If either slips, the revenue
target is not reachable through mid-market volume alone.

Third risk is support capacity during the migration window. Mitigation is to
hold the two approved support hires to a September start so they are ramped
before the cutover.
