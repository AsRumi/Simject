Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement - Summary

Agreement ID NDA-2214. The agreement is mutual; both parties are disclosing and
receiving parties, and the obligations are symmetrical.

Term. The confidentiality obligation runs for 3 years from the date of
disclosure, not from the date of signature. This distinction matters for
material disclosed late in the relationship.

Governing law is Delaware. Venue is exclusive to the Delaware state courts.

Carve-outs are the standard four: information already public, independently
developed, lawfully received from a third party, or required to be disclosed by
law or court order. The last carve-out requires prompt notice to the disclosing
party where notice is legally permitted.

There is no residuals clause, which is favourable to us.
