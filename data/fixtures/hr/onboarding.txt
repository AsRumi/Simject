New Hire Onboarding Checklist

Before day one. The hiring manager submits badge request form FRM-19 at least
five business days ahead, and confirms that the laptop order shows as shipped.

Day one. Complete the identity verification appointment, collect the badge, and
sign the standard acknowledgements. The new hire's buddy assignment is made by
the hiring manager and should be someone outside the new hire's direct reporting
line, so questions feel low stakes.

First week. Access to the four core systems, a walkthrough of the on-call
rotation even for non-engineering roles, and a scheduled thirty-minute meeting
with a stakeholder from a partner team.

First month. A written thirty-day check-in covering what is unclear, what is
blocked, and whether the role matches what was described in the interview.
