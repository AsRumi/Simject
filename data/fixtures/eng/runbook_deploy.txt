Deploy Runbook - billing-api

Preconditions. The change must be merged to main and the pipeline green. An
on-call approval is required before any production deploy; this is not optional
and is enforced by the pipeline, not by convention.

Steps.
1. Tag the release and let the pipeline build the image.
2. Deploy to canary at 5 percent of traffic and hold for fifteen minutes.
3. Watch error rate and p99 latency on the canary dashboard.
4. If both are within baseline, promote to 100 percent in two stages.

Rollback. Run the pipeline's rollback job against the previous tag. Rollback is
always preferred to a forward fix during an active incident. Do not attempt to
patch configuration by hand on running pods.
