Q3 Operations Review - Northeast Division

Support ticket volume rose 18 percent quarter over quarter, tracking the larger
installed base. Median first-response time improved from 6.1 hours to 4.4 hours
after the support headcount additions.

Headcount in operations specifically stood at 21 at quarter end. Attrition was
one person, who was backfilled in September.

Infrastructure spend was 240 thousand dollars, within the approved plan. The
planned migration of the reporting warehouse slipped to Q4; revenue recognition
was not affected.
