Paid Time Off Policy - version HR-POL-6

Accrual. Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off per month of
service, beginning on the first day of employment. Accrual is credited at the
end of each calendar month and is not prorated within a month.

Carryover. Unused time carries over into the following year up to a cap of 10
days. Anything above the cap is forfeited on January 1. The cap is not
adjustable by individual managers.

Requests. Time off is requested through the standard workflow and requires
manager approval. Requests of five or more consecutive days should be submitted
at least three weeks ahead so coverage can be arranged.

Separation. Accrued and unused time is paid out on separation where required by
state law.
