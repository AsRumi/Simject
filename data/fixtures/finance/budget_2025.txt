Budget Lines - Fiscal 2025, code BUD-2025-07

Capital expenditure is set at 1.1 million dollars, the majority of which is the
reporting warehouse migration and the storage expansion that depends on it.

Operating expense is flat year over year in absolute terms, which is a real
reduction once the approved compensation adjustments are counted. The reduction
is absorbed almost entirely in contractor spend rather than in tooling.

Headcount budget supports 6 additions, consistent with the Q4 operating plan.
The budget does not fund any role beyond that envelope, and requests above it
require a reforecast rather than a manager approval.

Infrastructure spend is budgeted at 240 thousand dollars per quarter.
