Reading Notes - agent evaluation

The most useful of the three papers is cited in our draft as smith2024agents.
Its contribution is methodological rather than empirical: it argues that agent
evaluations which score transcripts with a language model measure the grader's
disposition as much as the agent's behaviour, and proposes scoring against a
declared list of permitted operations instead.

The second paper reports strong results but evaluates on attacks the authors
wrote themselves, which makes the defense numbers hard to interpret. The authors
acknowledge this in the limitations section.

The third is a survey. Useful for its bibliography and for the taxonomy in
section three, less useful for its own claims.

Common thread worth stealing: all three separate the question of whether a
system produced bad text from whether it took a bad action.
